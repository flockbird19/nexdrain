      Device

      Health of the sensor box, the radio link and the receiver.

      How NEXDRAIN works

      A box under each tank lid warns while gas is still rising, before anyone opens the lid.

      1

      Sense

      A sealed box under the manhole lid reads the gas and the sewage level every second. It sounds its own buzzer if a limit is crossed, even with no signal.

      2

      Send

      Readings go by LoRa radio at 433 MHz to one receiver per site, every 5 seconds. A USB cable is the backup link.

      3

      Predict

      Statistical trend detection, not AI: it smooths each reading, measures how fast it is rising, and needs several readings to agree before it warns.

      4

      Warn

      The supervisor sees the tank status and a time-to-danger range. The worker at the lid sees one plain instruction: open, or do not open.

      Normal

      The box watches the closed tank. Nobody goes in. The supervisor sees the tank status and the time to danger.

      Cleaning

      The supervisor opens a permit after six checks. The attendant logs each person in and out, the crew taps "All OK" on a timer, and NEXDRAIN orders everyone out if the air changes.

      Maintenance

      The technician services the box. Alarm sounds pause for a fixed time only, every reading is still logged, and the worker screen says not to rely on it.

      • Oxygen
      • SMS alerts to the supervisor
      • A login per person
      • Several tanks per site
